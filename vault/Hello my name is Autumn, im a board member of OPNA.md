Hi my name is autumn and I am a OPNA board member and if being that may is bike month if you are wonder what is like to bike in Oak Park. Let show you. I have been consistantly biking as my main for of transportation for a majority of the last 10 years I even got rid of my car a few years ago to share one with my partner. 4 of those years have been in Oak Park. While not always perfect, roads like stockton and broadway lacking bike infrastruture can make it hard to get around and the freeway underpasses often make going places outside of oak park less comfortable than one would like. Over all it has been a wonderful experience that is only been getting better. Some of my favorite parts about biking have been forming a better connection to the layout of oak park, never having to deal with parking and often getting to park right in front of stores. Knowing that I am reducing traffic and parking for those who need to drive. Knowing I am doing my part for the environment. And one of the best right now is not having to worry about gas prices. Now I don't expect anyone to start biking at my level, but I think it is something worth trying and right now is a perfect time to do so.
